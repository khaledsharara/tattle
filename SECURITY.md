# Security

Tattle runs on your home network and keeps sign-ins to your internet provider,
so security reports are welcome and taken seriously.

**Please don't open a public issue for a vulnerability.** Use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/khaledsharara/tattle/security/advisories/new)
instead, with what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version.

What's in scope: the dashboard and its API, the gateway container, the
installer, and the provider and router plugins (for example, a way to read
stored sign-ins, or to change settings without the admin password).

What Tattle does to keep you safe, and what it expects from you, is in
[Privacy and security](README.md#privacy-and-security).
